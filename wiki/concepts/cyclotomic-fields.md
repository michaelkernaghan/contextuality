# Cyclotomic Fields

## Definition

A cyclotomic field Q(ζ_n) is the field extension of the rationals obtained by adjoining a primitive nth root of unity ζ_n = e^{2πi/n}. The ring of integers of Q(ζ_n) is the ring Z[ζ_n]. In the context of KS constructions, a **cyclotomic coordinate alphabet** is the set of nth roots of unity used as vector entries: S_n = {ζ_n^k : k = 0, …, n−1}. The ray set generated by taking all unit vectors in C^3 with entries in S_n ∪ {0} is the object whose KS-uncolorability is studied.

## Key Results

**The 6|n theorem** ([[cyclotomic-letter]]): The ray set S_n is KS-uncolorable if and only if 6 divides n.

The proof decomposes into two independent mechanisms:
- **Phase cancellation** (3|n): the identity 1 + ω + ω^2 = 0 (ω = e^{2πi/3}) allows vanishing 3-term sums of roots of unity that form triads. Handled by a projective collapse lemma showing all-nonzero rays form isolated 3-cycles when 3|n but 2 does not divide n.
- **Interlocking** (2|n): the element −1 ∈ S_n creates two-term vanishing sums (ζ^a + ζ^b = 0 iff 2|n) that link triads into uncolorable configurations. Handled by a plane matching lemma.

Both conditions are necessary: 6 = 2 × 3, and neither prime alone suffices for uncolorability.

A subtlety noted in [[cyclotomic-letter]]: Q(ζ_3) = Q(ζ_6) as fields, yet S_3 is colorable while S_6 is not. The relevant structure is the available root subgroup μ_n, not the ambient field.

The result is consistent with but logically independent of the Cortez-Morales-Reyes ring theorem that Z[1/6] is the minimal ring supporting KS contextuality in dimension 3 ([[algebraic-islands-main]]).

## In Our Work

The cyclotomic family is the algebraic island for which we have the sharpest and most complete characterization. The 6|n theorem is the one result in our program that is a complete classification rather than an empirical observation: every cyclotomic alphabet is handled with an explicit algebraic proof. The Eisenstein island (Z[ω], 6th roots of unity) is the simplest case within this family and yields the Cabello 33-vector KS set as the minimal example.

## Open Questions

- Does the projective collapse argument generalize to Gaussian integers Z[i] or other quadratic integer alphabets?
- Is there a unified proof covering both the cyclotomic 6|n theorem and the modulus-2 cancellation boundary?
- For which n does S_n contain a KS set with exactly 33 vectors (the Eisenstein minimum), and for which n is the minimum higher?
- Can a direct algebraic bridge be constructed between the 6|n result and the Cortez-Morales-Reyes Z[1/6] theorem?
