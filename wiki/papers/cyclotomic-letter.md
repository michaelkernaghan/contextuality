# Kochen-Specker Uncolorability in Root-of-Unity Coordinate Alphabets Requires Exactly 6|n (Kernaghan, 2026)

## Summary

This PRL-format letter proves a sharp characterization theorem for [[kochen-specker-theorem|KS]] sets constructed from nth roots of unity: the ray set S_n generated by the nth roots of unity in C^3 is KS-uncolorable if and only if 6 divides n. The result confirms that the primes 2 and 3 are both necessary for [[contextuality|KS-uncolorability]] in the [[cyclotomic-fields|cyclotomic]] coordinate family, and supplies a complete algebraic proof with explicit colorings for the non-uncolorable cases.

The sufficiency direction is straightforward: if 6|n, then the 6th roots of unity embed in the nth roots, so Cabello's Eisenstein 33-vector [[ks-set]] (which lives in S_6) embeds in S_n, and KS-uncolorability is monotone. The necessity direction is the main technical contribution: a careful analysis of short vanishing sums of roots of unity (at most 3-term sums arise as orthogonality conditions in C^3) shows that when 6 does not divide n, an explicit valid KS coloring can always be constructed.

Three cases are handled: when neither 2 nor 3 divides n, the only triad is the axis triad (trivially colorable); when 2|n but 3 does not divide n, triads only involve axis rays and matched pairs of one-zero rays in coordinate planes, and an explicit plane-by-plane coloring works; when 3|n but 2 does not divide n, a projective collapse argument shows that all-nonzero triads decompose into isolated, pairwise-disjoint triangles, making the coloring problem fully separable. The theorem's 6 = 2 x 3 factorization mirrors the ring-theoretic result of Cortez-Morales-Reyes that Z[1/6] is the minimal ring supporting KS contextuality in dimension 3, though the two results are logically independent.

## Key Claims

- Theorem (main): S_n is KS-uncolorable if and only if 6|n
- Lemma (3-term vanishing sums): if u, v, w are nth roots of unity and u+v+w=0, then 3|n and {u,v,w} = {lambda, lambda*omega, lambda*omega^2} for some nth root lambda; conversely, 3|n suffices
- Lemma (2-term vanishing sums): zeta^a + zeta^b = 0 has a solution if and only if 2|n
- Lemma (projective collapse): for odd n with 3|n, every all-nonzero ray has exactly two orthogonal all-nonzero neighbors, and these form isolated 3-cycles under T_+: v -> (1, omega*zeta^p, omega^2*zeta^q)
- Lemma (plane matching): for even n, the orthogonality graph on one-zero rays in each coordinate plane is a perfect matching (each ray has exactly one orthogonal partner at phase distance n/2)
- The factorization 6 = 2x3 reflects two independent mechanisms: phase cancellation (3|n creates triads) and interlocking (2|n links triads via the -1 element); both are necessary for uncolorability
- The result is consistent with but logically independent of the Cortez-Morales-Reyes ring theorem about Z[1/6]
- Note: Q(zeta_3) = Q(zeta_6) as fields, yet S_3 is colorable while S_6 is not; the relevant structure is the available root subgroup mu_n, not the ambient field
- Numerical cross-check: all coloring constructions verified for n <= 100 using PySAT/Glucose4

## Methods

- Classification of ray types by zero pattern: axis rays (2 zero coordinates), one-zero rays (1 zero), all-nonzero rays (0 zeros)
- Lam-Leung theory of vanishing sums of roots of unity (only short sums needed here)
- Projective collapse argument: normalizing the inner product to identify the exact two all-nonzero neighbors of any all-nonzero ray when 3|n
- Explicit constructive colorings for all three non-uncolorable cases (Cases 1, 2, 3)
- SAT-based numerical verification for n <= 100 (Glucose4 solver via PySAT)
- Monotonicity of KS-uncolorability (superset of uncolorable set is uncolorable) for the sufficiency direction
