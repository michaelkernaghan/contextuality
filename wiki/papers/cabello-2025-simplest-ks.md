# Simplest Kochen-Specker Set

## Summary

This paper establishes the new record for the smallest KS set in dimension 3 (d=3, qutrit): a **33-vector, 14-basis** set that is uncolorable and state-independently contextual. This beats the previous record of 33 vectors in 16 bases (Peres, 1991 — [[peres-33-3d]]). The construction derives from the **Yu-Oh 13-ray set** augmented by the action of the **Weyl-Heisenberg (WH) group** in dimension 3. The set is unique up to unitary equivalence. A key application: the 14-basis set enables a **5×9 qutrit-qutrit bipartite perfect quantum strategy** (pseudotelepathy), refuting Conjecture 2 of [[cabello-2025-bipartite]] (which predicted 9×7 as the simplest).

## Core Results

### Theorem 1: Existence of the 33-vector, 14-basis KS Set

There exists a set of 33 unit vectors in ℂ³ forming 14 orthogonal bases (triples) with no valid KS coloring. This is the smallest known KS set in d=3 by the number of bases (previous: 16 bases).

### Theorem 2: Uniqueness

The 33-vector, 14-basis set is unique up to unitary equivalence — there is essentially only one such minimal set at this vector count and basis count.

### Corollary: 5×9 Qutrit-Qutrit Perfect Strategy

From the 14-basis KS set, one constructs a two-player nonlocal game with 5 inputs for Alice and 9 inputs for Bob (|X|=5, |Y|=9), sharing a qutrit-qutrit entangled state, that is won with probability 1 by the quantum strategy and with probability < 1 by any classical strategy. This is the simplest known qutrit-qutrit BPQS — refuting Conjecture 2 of [[cabello-2025-bipartite]], which predicted the simplest would be 9×7.

## Construction

### Starting Point: Yu-Oh 13-Ray Set

The Yu-Oh set is a state-**dependent** contextuality proof in d=3: 13 rays, no KS coloring for specific quantum states. It is not state-independent (not a KS set in the strict sense).

### Weyl-Heisenberg Group Action

The Weyl-Heisenberg group in dimension d is generated by the shift X and clock Z operators:
- X|j⟩ = |j+1 mod d⟩
- Z|j⟩ = ω^j|j⟩, where ω = e^{2πi/d}

Acting with WH on the Yu-Oh rays generates additional rays. The closure of the Yu-Oh set under WH group action in d=3 produces the 33-vector set.

### Majorana Representation

The vectors are verified and visualized using the **Majorana stellar representation** of qutrit states on the Bloch sphere (CP² → S²): each qutrit ray corresponds to a pair of points on S², enabling geometric intuition for the coloring impossibility.

## Connection to SIC-POVMs

The Weyl-Heisenberg group is the symmetry group of **symmetric informationally complete positive operator-valued measures (SIC-POVMs)**. The appearance of WH symmetry in the construction connects to deep questions about SIC-POVM existence — a theme in [[algebraic-islands-main]] and [[cyclotomic-fields]] (Zauner's conjecture; SIC-POVM existence over cyclotomic fields).
