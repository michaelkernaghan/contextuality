# Archived letters (not current)

Five short letters written in March and April 2026 alongside versions 1-9 of *The Algebraic
Landscape of Kochen-Specker Sets in Dimension Three*. They are kept here as a record of earlier
work and because other documents refer to them. **They have not been revised and are not
current.** Versions 1-9 of the paper are withdrawn, and these letters rest on the same
classification. Do not cite a claim from them without checking it against version 11 of the
paper ([`paper/algebraic_islands_v11.pdf`](../../paper/algebraic_islands_v11.pdf)) and the
status ledger ([`LEDGER.md`](../../LEDGER.md)).

| File | Subject | Status against version 11 |
|---|---|---|
| `cyclotomic_letter` | Root-of-unity pools are uncolourable exactly when 6 divides n | The theorem stands; it is Section 5 of version 11 and is checked in Lean for n ≥ 3 (`lean/Cyclotomic6n.lean`). The letter's text is the older write-up. |
| `heegner7_letter` | The 43-ray set over Q(√−7) and the 52-ray golden-ratio set, described as new, with "contextual advantage" figures | The 43-ray set is uncolourable and is the minimum of its pool (Lean). Withdrawn: 52 as the golden-ratio minimum (a 51-ray set exists), the claims of novelty, and the advantage figures. |
| `universality_letter` | 31-ray sets from several alphabets share one orthogonality graph | "Graph universality" is withdrawn as a general statement. |
| `sub31_letter` | Computational evidence that no KS set has fewer than 31 rays | Withdrawn as stated: the "OCUS" certification was unsound and "merge saturation" is withdrawn. Version 11 proves 31 as a lower bound only inside the alphabet families it classifies. |
| `galois_letter` | "Galois-theoretic classification of Kochen-Specker sets in dimension three" | Never part of the paper; not re-checked. `galois_letter_backup_v7_pre_fix.tex` is an earlier draft. |

The entries in the last column summarise Appendix A of version 11 and the ledger. The letters
themselves were not re-read line by line when this note was written.
